## Resultados possíveis

### Money decisions (money)

Make your next money decision with clarity.

A money worry can turn into postponing a decision or avoiding the numbers altogether. Start with one decision. Separate what you know from what you fear, then choose an action you can take.

What money decision am I avoiding, and what do I need to make it?

### Time for yourself (capacity)

Make room for your own plans.

Being dependable can leave little room for what you want. Start with one plan you’ve put aside. Decide what time it needs and whether there’s a request you can decline, defer or ask someone to share.

What am I putting aside for others, and what boundary could make room for it?

### Other people’s expectations (expectations)

Make the choice that’s yours.

Old expectations can make a decision feel settled before you’ve asked what you want. Pick one “should” about work, money or relationships. Consider whether it fits your life now and what you would choose.

Which “should” is shaping this decision, and what do I actually want?

### Catch the thought. Choose your response.

A missed plan can turn into “I never follow through.” A request can get an automatic yes. A money worry can become another week of avoiding a decision.

Choose a recent example. Write down what happened, what you told yourself and what you did next. Include the practical constraints, such as time, money or other responsibilities.

### If you get stuck

**What if I don’t know why I do this?**

You can leave that question open. Write about something you remember and what you noticed at the time. You don’t need an explanation for everything.

**What if I can’t do what I planned?**

Choose a smaller step or a different time. If you planned to review your spending, opening a bill might be enough to get started. Adjust the plan to what’s possible.

**How do I know when I’m done?**

You’re done when you’ve described the situation and chosen something to try next. You can come back to your notes afterward and see what you’d change.

**Where is my writing saved?**

The form does not send your writing anywhere or keep it after the page is reloaded. Use “Download my reflection” to save a text file on your device.
