### Carrossel · Uma prática completa em seis telas

1. **Before your next automatic yes.**
2. **Pause the answer.** “Let me check what I can take on.”
3. **Look at what is already there.** What would this new request require you to move?
4. **Choose what you can offer.** It might be the whole task, part of it, another time, or a conversation about priorities.
5. **Prepare the words.** Write the response you want to use. Adapt it to the person and situation.
6. **Try the first Protocol 44 exercise.** Choose a moment from your week and prepare a next step. Free through the link in our profile.

Legenda: “A starting practice for manageable, everyday requests. You choose where it fits. Another person’s response is their own.”
