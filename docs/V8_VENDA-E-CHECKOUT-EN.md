### Work on the moments where your choices get difficult.

The first exercise gives you a situation to work with. The program helps you examine it further and practice a response you want to use.

**The Reading · Video sessions 1–9**

Notice what happens, separate an event from your interpretation, and examine the rules and expectations that show up in your choices.

**You’ll work toward:** a specific response and a situation in which to try it.

Apply what you have been practicing to everyday decisions. Review what happens after progress or interruption, and prepare a way to continue.

## A closer look at the practice cards

### Have words ready before the next request arrives.

“Let me check what I can take on and get back to you.”

That is one starting phrase. The card asks you to adapt it to your situation, use it where appropriate, and review what happened.

Other cards cover competing work tasks, offering part of the help requested, protecting an evening, sharing a task, asking for space in a conversation and revising an agreement.

You choose the words and the situation. Another person’s response remains their own.

## Is this a good fit?

This program is for adults who want to understand a recurring situation and practice handling it differently. You should be willing to write, reflect, try an action and review it.

You can use it with an everyday decision or boundary. You don’t need to explain your entire life or identify the origin of every response.

Protocol 44 is educational self-reflection. It does not diagnose conditions, provide therapy or guarantee financial or relationship outcomes.
